OVERVIEW


A gym is a place for people to exercise and get fit,an can include a variety of equipments and amentities:

Equipments

Gyms typically have exercise machines, free weights, and benches and stands for those weights. Some gyms also have exercise bikes and running machines.

Purpose

Gyms can help people reach their fitness goals, whether that's building strength or toning up. People can also use gyms to improve their technique, connect with others, and achieve their goals.

Health Requirements

Before starting a gym routine, people may be asked to disclose any medical conditions, such as heart conditions, diabetes, or major operations. This helps personal trainers create a routine that's right for the individual.
